Modeling
Move onto the centre dial to see the hover state. Click it to go back or close; click anywhere to reopen. Slow 1.2 s slows open and close too.
Rest
Bearing ring, ticks and needle as in variant G. The needle follows the pointer.
Hover
Signal-red disc with a 3 px white border in place of the needle, its radius 75% of the needle length. The ring stays, the outer arrow slides onto the ring, shrinks and fades, and the white ticks shrink to the length of the grey ones.
Between
The needle shrinks into the centre and fades while the disc grows out of it and the white ticks shorten. 120 ms, eased in and out; leaving mid-way reverses from where it is.
Open, close
Every element fades. Slots and labels travel out from 12% further in, the dial grows from 92%. 120 ms each way, ease-out.
Slot width
At least 120 px; grows to fit its label with 12 px padding each side, up to 180 px. A longer label is clipped at the padding. On hover it waits 400 ms, then slides left at 40 px/s until its end shows, and eases back when the pointer leaves. Knife Project From View tests this.
Placement
One rule for any number of slots: the box sits on the ring at its bearing and moves out by half its width times the bearing's sine and half its height times its cosine. East and west boxes start at the ring, north and south sit above and below it, and diagonals clear their neighbours. Twelve slots use a ring 1.4 times wider. Current shows the 8-slot special cases for comparison.
12-slot spacing
Boxes are wide and short. With equal angles, neighbours near north and south sit side by side and their gap is their distance minus 120 px; near east and west they stack and the gap is their distance minus 32 px. Equal gaps moves the bearings to 0, 42, 68, 90, 112, 138, 180 and their mirrors, on a 170 px ring, so each pair of neighbours is 37 px apart.
Sub-pie slot
Stone outline, grey proximity highlight. Resting on it wipes signal orange across it, away from the centre (east slots left to right, west right to left, north upward, south downward), over the sub-pie delay; the sub-pie opens when it is full.
Sub-pie
The parent pie fades and shrinks to 90% toward the pointer while the sub-pie opens there, both in the same 120 ms. Back reverses it: the sub-pie shrinks to 88% around its own centre and fades as the parent opens.
Opening
A pie or sub-pie opens under the pointer with the needle showing. The centre hover arms only after the pointer has left the dial ring once.
Closing
The dial keeps the state it had when closing began: closed from the centre, it fades out as the disc, and the needle does not return.
Hit area
The dial's outer ring, the same area that already runs back or close.
